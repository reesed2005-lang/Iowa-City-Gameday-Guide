A/B Test Plan — Iowa City Game Day Guide

1. Page and Element Being Tested

For my A/B test, I will test two different headlines on the homepage of my Iowa City Game Day Guide website. I want to see which headline makes visitors more interested in exploring my website.

Version A (Original): Iowa City Game Day Guide: Make Every Hawkeye Game Count

Version B (New): First Iowa Football Game? Your Complete Guide to Parking, Food, and Game Day Traditions

The only difference between the two versions will be the main headline. Everything else will remain the same.

2. Hypothesis

I predict that Version B will receive higher interest ratings because it specifically addresses first-time Iowa football visitors and highlights the information they need. This could make visitors more likely to explore the website.

3. AI Model, Persona, and Procedure

I will use ChatGPT GPT-6 with the same settings for every trial.

Each AI visitor will represent someone attending their first Iowa Hawkeyes football game who needs information about parking, restaurants, and game day traditions.

Each trial will take place in a new ChatGPT conversation. I will randomly assign 50 trials to Version A and 50 to Version B before testing.

Exact prompt:

"You are attending your first Iowa Hawkeyes football game and need help planning your visit. Read the following homepage and rate how likely you would be to continue exploring this website on a scale of 1–100, where 1 means extremely unlikely and 100 means extremely likely. Respond with only a number.

Homepage:
[Insert the full homepage text for the assigned version.]"

4. Primary Outcome

The primary outcome will be the likelihood rating from 1–100. I will calculate the average rating for each version.

5. Sample Size and Timeline

I will conduct 100 trials total, with 50 trials for each version. I plan to finish collecting responses by November 24, 2026. I will complete all trials regardless of the results.

6. Comparison and Success Criteria

I will compare the average ratings for Version A and Version B using a two-sample t-test. My hypothesis will be supported if Version B receives a higher average rating and the p-value is below 0.05.

7. SEO Protection

Version B will have a noindex tag and will not be included in my sitemap so that it does not compete with the original homepage in Google search results.
