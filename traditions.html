Iowa City Game Day Guide

Iowa Football Tailgating & Traditions

Get ready for the atmosphere around Kinnick Stadium with tailgating planning tips and an introduction to the Iowa Wave.

Be part of the atmosphere

A game day can be more than the action on the field. Fans gather with friends and family, wear black and gold, and share traditions that shape the experience.

Tradition

The Iowa Wave

At the end of the first quarter, fans at Kinnick Stadium turn toward the nearby children’s hospital and wave in a shared gesture of support.

Pregame

Tailgating together

Plan food, water, weather-ready clothing, and a meeting point. Follow all current university and parking-area policies.

Inside Kinnick

Cheering with the crowd

Wear your colors, arrive in time to find your seat, and enjoy the energy of the stadium while respecting fellow fans.

A simple first-timer checklist

Confirm the kickoff time, check the forecast, review current stadium entry and bag policies, and decide where to meet your group. If you are tailgating, verify which activities are permitted in your chosen location.

Remember: Rules and schedules can change. Use Iowa Athletics for current event information.
